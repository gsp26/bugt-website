Bugt privacy notice

Your budget is personal.
Your data should be, too.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Data stored by Bugt

Bugt stores expenses, transaction categories, budget settings, and imported transaction text locally on your device. This information is used to show your transaction history, category summaries, and budget progress.

Optional SMS transaction import

If you enable automatic import on Android, Bugt requests access to read your SMS inbox and detect new incoming SMS messages. Bugt checks message text on your device to identify financial transaction alerts. Only messages recognized as transactions are added to your expense history; the imported transaction includes the original alert text so you can review it.

SMS access is optional. You can decline and continue using manual entry or paste/share import. You can revoke SMS access in Android Settings under Apps, Bugt, Permissions. If Bugt receives transaction alerts while closed, it queues potential transaction messages locally and processes them when you reopen the app.

Bugt does not send SMS contents or expense data to a Bugt server. Bugt has no account or cloud-sync feature. Android device backup may restore app data according to your device backup settings.

Sharing and retention

Bugt does not sell or share your transaction data. Your local app data remains on the device until you delete transactions, clear Bugt’s app data, or uninstall Bugt. Some Android backup settings may retain app data for device restoration.

Children’s privacy

Bugt is a personal budgeting tool and is not designed specifically for children.

Contact

For questions about Bugt or this notice, use the contact details shown on the Bugt listing in Google Play.
